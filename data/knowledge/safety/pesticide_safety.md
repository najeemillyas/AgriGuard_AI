Never invent a pesticide dose, tank-mix compatibility, legal approval, waiting period or guarantee. Use only a current product label and locally applicable professional guidance. Protect applicators, bystanders, animals, pollinators and water. Escalate unsupported crops, severe rapid damage, conflicting evidence, exposure incidents, unverified chemical requests and cases with inadequate evidence.
