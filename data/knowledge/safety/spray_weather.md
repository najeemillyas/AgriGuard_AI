Do not recommend immediate spraying when rain is likely to wash off the application or when wind can cause drift. The prototype treats rain probability of 60 percent or more or wind speed of 25 kilometres per hour or more as unsuitable. Rain from 35 to 59 percent or wind from 18 to 24 kilometres per hour requires caution. The product label and local professional guidance take precedence.
