title: Chilli Fungal Leaf Spot Guidance
crop: chilli
problem: fungal_disease
source: AgriGuard Approved Chilli Guide
version: 1.0
---
Fungal leaf spots can cause discrete brown lesions, yellow halos and premature leaf fall during wet or humid periods. Remove affected debris, improve plant spacing and keep leaves dry where possible. Do not confuse insect feeding or bacterial symptoms with fungal disease. Verify diagnosis before selecting any crop-protection product.
