title: Chilli Thrips Guidance
crop: chilli
problem: insect_attack
source: AgriGuard Approved Chilli Guide
version: 1.0
---
Thrips feeding may produce upward leaf curl, silvery scars, distorted new growth and flower damage. Inspect young leaves and flowers closely because mites, viruses and environmental stress can produce overlapping symptoms. Use field sanitation and monitoring. Apply only locally approved controls supported by the product label and expert confirmation.
