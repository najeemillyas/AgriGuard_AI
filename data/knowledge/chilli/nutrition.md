Interveinal yellowing or general chlorosis may be related to nutrient availability, pH, waterlogging, root injury or salinity. Note which leaves are affected first and review irrigation and fertiliser history. Laboratory soil or tissue analysis gives stronger evidence than visual symptoms alone.
