---
title: Tomato Fruit Borer Guidance
crop: tomato
problem: insect_attack
source: AgriGuard Approved Tomato Guide
version: 1.0
---
Fruit borer larvae may feed on leaves, buds and fruits and can leave entry holes with waste material. Inspect flowers and fruits and remove visibly infested fruits. Use monitoring traps and conserve beneficial organisms. Chemical control should be based on field confirmation, current local recommendations and label directions.
