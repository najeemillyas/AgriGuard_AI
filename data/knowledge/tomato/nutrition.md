title: Tomato Nutrient Symptom Guidance
crop: tomato

source: AgriGuard Approved Tomato Guide

---
Yellowing and weak growth can arise from nutrient imbalance, root damage, water stress, pH or disease. Record whether symptoms begin on older or younger leaves and whether they are uniform or interveinal. Check irrigation and root condition. Confirm suspected deficiency with soil or tissue testing before making a large corrective application.
