---
title: Tomato Early Blight Guidance
crop: tomato
problem: fungal_disease
source: AgriGuard Approved Tomato Guide
version: 1.0
---
Early blight commonly produces brown leaf lesions with concentric rings, often beginning on older leaves. Remove badly affected lower leaves, keep foliage off the soil, improve airflow and avoid overhead irrigation late in the day. Rotate crops and remove infected residues. Use a fungicide only when its current label covers tomato and the diagnosed disease.
