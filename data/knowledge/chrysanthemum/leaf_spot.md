Fungal leaf spots may appear as brown or dark lesions that expand during humid conditions. Remove infected debris, improve ventilation and avoid prolonged leaf wetness. Compare symptoms across old and new leaves and inspect for insect damage before concluding. Fungicide use requires verified crop and disease label coverage; this guide does not supply a dosage.
