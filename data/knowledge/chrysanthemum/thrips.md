title: Chrysanthemum Thrips Field Guidance
crop: chrysanthemum
problem: insect_attack
source: AgriGuard Approved Chrysanthemum Guide
version: 1.0
---
Thrips may cause distorted petals, brown streaking, silvery feeding marks and small insects inside flowers. Inspect several flowers and leaf undersides before deciding. Remove badly damaged flowers and weeds that may host pests. Use sticky traps for monitoring. If intervention is necessary, use only a locally permitted product whose label explicitly covers the crop and target pest. Avoid spraying during strong wind, likely rain or peak pollinator activity. Rotate approved modes of action and follow label precautions.
