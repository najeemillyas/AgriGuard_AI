title: Chrysanthemum Aphid Field Guidance
crop: chrysanthemum
problem: insect_attack
source: AgriGuard Approved Chrysanthemum Guide
version: 1.0
---
Aphids cluster on tender shoots and flower stems and may cause curling, sticky honeydew and sooty mould. Confirm colonies on multiple plants. Remove severely infested tips where practical, control weeds and conserve natural enemies. Avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum spraying. Any pesticide decision must follow the current label, local approval and agricultural expert advice.
