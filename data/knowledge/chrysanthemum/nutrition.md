Uniform yellowing, interveinal chlorosis or poor growth may indicate nutrient stress, root stress, unsuitable pH or waterlogging. Compare old and young leaves and review recent fertiliser and irrigation records. Do not diagnose a single deficiency from colour alone. Soil, water or leaf testing is preferred before corrective fertiliser application.
